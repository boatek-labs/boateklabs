Privacy Policy

What we collect through this site, why, and how it's handled.

Home » Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2026

What we collect

When you use the contact form, newsletter signup, or the Signal Capsule chat on this site, we collect the information you provide directly: your name, email address, and the message or project details you submit. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

How we use it

  • Contact form submissions are used to respond to your project inquiry and are stored so we can follow up.
  • Newsletter signups are used only to send occasional studio updates and devlogs. You can ask to be removed at any time by emailing us.
  • Signal Capsule messages are used to respond to your question and are stored alongside contact submissions for context.

How it's stored

Submissions are stored securely on our own server infrastructure. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. We do not use your information for advertising.

Your rights

You can request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time. Contact us using the details on our Contact page to make this request.

Cookies

This site uses your browser's local storage (not cookies) to remember your dark/light theme preference. This stays on your device and is never sent to our server.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Significant changes will be noted on this page.
