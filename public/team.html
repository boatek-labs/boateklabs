Our Story

Boatek Labs started as a founder writing code alone, and grew into a small studio that treats software, games, and digital experiences as one connected discipline rather than three separate businesses. We are not positioning ourselves as an AI company — AI is a tool we use when it serves the work, not the point of the work itself.

Vision & Mission

To be the studio people think of first when they need something built properly — whether that's a piece of software people use every day, a game people finish and remember, or an experience that doesn't fit a neat category.
